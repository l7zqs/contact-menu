About

I spend most of my time in the gap between design and implementation — the part where a mockup has to survive real content, real screen sizes, and real people clicking things too fast. Lately that's meant a lot of work on small, dependency-free components that a website can drop in without inheriting someone else's build pipeline.

Before going independent, I led the design-systems team at a logistics startup, and before that spent three years making maps render faster than they had any right to.
